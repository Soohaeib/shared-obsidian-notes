# Comprehensive Syllabus Mapping Tracker
> [!info] How to use this note
> This document maps your 5 courses to the specific textbooks provided. Use the tables to guide your daily reading.
> - **Chapters & Sections to Read:** Tells you exactly which heading/lesson to focus on.
> - **Omit / Skip:** Tells you what to ignore so you don't waste time on irrelevant material (e.g., US-specific tax laws, matrix algebra, complex appendices). *(Note: ACC 302 does not have a skip list per your instructor's guidance).*
> - **Local Supplements:** Indicates where you need to rely on your professor's class notes or local Bangladeshi laws.

## ACC 305: Auditing and Assurance (Hybrid Approach)
**Core Textbooks:** *Auditing and Assurance Services* by Messier (Foreign) **&** *Contemporary Auditing* by Mahmud & Uddin (Local)
**Syllabus Coverage:** 100%

> [!tip] Hybrid Study Strategy
> - **Use Messier (Foreign):** For clear definitions, understanding core logic (like how the Audit Risk Model works), and visualizing business process workflows (Revenue, Inventory, etc.).
> - **Use Local Book:** For memorizing the specific clauses of the **Companies Act 1994**, the **ICAB Code of Ethics**, and the exact structures of **ISAs** (International Standards on Auditing) required by your syllabus.
