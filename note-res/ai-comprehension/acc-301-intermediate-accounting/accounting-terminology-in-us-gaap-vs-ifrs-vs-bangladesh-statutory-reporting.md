# ACC 301 Comprehensive Accounting Terminology Reference & Statutory Lookup Guide
### 1. Introduction & Comparative Framework Architecture
#### 1.1 Purpose & Navigation Guide
This reference guide provides a technical cross-framework lookup for students enrolled in **ACC 301: Intermediate Accounting**. The objective of this document is to eliminate nomenclature confusion and conceptual ambiguity across three distinct reporting regimes studied in the curriculum:
1. **US GAAP:** The United States Generally Accepted Accounting Principles, governed by the Financial Accounting Standards Board (FASB) Accounting Standards Codification (FASB ASC) and illustrated in primary course texts (Weygandt, Kieso, and Warfield).
2. **IFRS:** International Financial Reporting Standards, issued by the International Accounting Standards Board (IASB), representing the standard class norm across international jurisdictions (IAS/IFRS).
3. **Bangladesh Statutory Framework:** The legal and regulatory mandates governing corporate entities in Bangladesh, primarily embodied in **The Companies Act, 1994 (Schedule XI)** and the **Securities and Exchange Rules, 1987 (Rule 12(2))** under the jurisdiction of the Bangladesh Securities and Exchange Commission (BSEC).

To navigate this guide effectively, students should utilise the comparative lookup tables in each section to identify precise equivalent terms, explicit presentation order requirements, and underlying recognition variances.

#### 2.2 Qualitative Characteristics & Conceptual Terms
- **Relevance & Faithful Representation Nuances:**
    - **US GAAP (SFAC No. 8):** Defines fundamental qualities as _Relevance_ (Predictive Value, Confirmatory Value, Materiality) and _Faithful Representation_ (Completeness, Neutrality, Free from Error). Conservatism/Prudence was explicitly omitted from qualitative characteristics to preserve neutrality and eliminate systematic understatement bias.
    - **IFRS Framework:** Re-introduced _Prudence_ (defined as the exercise of caution when making judgements under conditions of uncertainty) to directly support neutrality and stewardship reporting.
- **Analytical Depth — Status & Operational Impact of Framework Documents:**
    - **US GAAP:** _Statements of Financial Accounting Concepts (SFACs)_ are non-authoritative. They guide board members during standard formulation but cannot be cited by preparers to override specific rules in the FASB Accounting Standards Codification (FASB ASC). When resolving novel accounting issues, preparers must look to analogous ASC Topics within the authoritative hierarchy.
    - **IFRS:** The _IFRS Conceptual Framework for Financial Reporting_ is authoritative guidance. Under IAS 8, in the absence of a specific IFRS or IAS standard addressing a transaction, management is explicitly required to refer to and apply the Conceptual Framework to develop an accounting policy that results in relevant and reliable information.

#### 3.2 Valuation & Exam Rule Notes
- **Schedule XI Sundry Debtors Classification & Presentation Mandate:** Part I of Schedule XI explicitly requires _Sundry Debtors_ to be segregated and disclosed in the notes or face of the balance sheet into three mandatory legal categories:
    1. Debts considered good and in respect of which the company is fully secured.
    2. Debts considered good for which the company holds no security other than the debtor's personal security (unsecured).
    3. Debts considered doubtful or bad and not provided for.
    - _Operational Presentation Mechanics:_ Under Schedule XI Part I, the _Provision for Bad and Doubtful Debts_ is explicitly deducted directly on the face of the asset section under _Sundry Debtors_. Conversely, bad debts that have been explicitly written off during the period reduce the gross debtors balance prior to applying the provision deduction.
- **Impairment Model Divergence (Incurred vs. Expected Credit Loss):**
    - **US GAAP (Historical Incurred Loss Model / ASC 326 CECL):** Historically evaluated allowance based on incurred loss events, transitioning to Current Expected Credit Loss (CECL) estimating lifetime expected losses over contractual terms.
    - **IFRS 9 (Expected Credit Loss - ECL Framework):** Uses a forward-looking 3-stage ECL model. Stage 1 requires a 12-month ECL assessment immediately upon asset recognition, moving to Stage 2/3 lifetime ECL if credit risk increases significantly.

#### 4.2 Statutory Disclosures & Method Constraints
**Bangladesh Statutory Inventory Mandate — Schedule XI, Part II** Under Part II of Schedule XI (Companies Act, 1994), manufacturing entities must disclose specific quantitative inventory details in the financial statement notes:
1. **Quantitative Raw Material Breakdown:** Disaggregated quantities and values of major raw materials consumed during the accounting period.
2. **Imported vs. Indigenous Materials:** Clear disclosure distinguishing between the value of imported raw materials and spare parts versus indigenous (locally sourced) raw materials and spare parts.
3. **Opening and Closing Balance Verification:** Physical quantities and total valuations of opening and closing stock for each major category of finished goods, work-in-progress, and raw materials.

#### 6.2 Fair Value Hierarchy & Statutory Valuation Notes
- **Three-Level Fair Value Hierarchy (US GAAP ASC 820 / IFRS 13):**
    - **Level 1 (Least Subjective):** Quoted prices in active markets for _identical_ assets or liabilities that the entity can access at the measurement date (_e.g._, 2,000 shares of General Electric [GE] common stock trading actively on the New York Stock Exchange).
    - **Level 2 (Moderately Subjective):** Inputs other than quoted Level 1 prices that are observable directly or indirectly (_e.g._, unquoted local development authority bonds valued using yield curves on benchmark Treasury bonds of similar duration).
    - **Level 3 (Most Subjective):** Unobservable inputs reflecting management's own assumptions and internal valuation models (_e.g._, equity shares in Sastre Company, a private start-up entity, valued using internal discounted cash flow [DCF] projections).
- **Statutory Investment Reporting under Schedule XI:** Schedule XI Part I requires investments to be listed at cost, with mandatory footnote disclosure showing the aggregate market value of quoted investments and the explicit valuation basis used for unquoted holdings.

#### 7.2 Recognition Thresholds & Exam Pitfalls
- **Accrual Recognition Thresholds (Loss Contingencies vs. Provisions):**
    - **US GAAP (ASC 450):** Requires accrual if the loss is **Probable** (interpreted as a high threshold, \sim 75\% \text{--} 80\%+ likelihood) AND reasonably estimable. If a range of loss is estimated and no point in the range is better than any other, **accrue the MINIMUM amount** in the range and disclose the remaining financial exposure in the notes.
    - **IFRS (IAS 37):** Requires accrual of a Provision if the outflow is **More Likely Than Not** (> 50\% probability). If a range is estimated, **accrue the MIDPOINT (expected value)** of the range.
- **Short-Term Debt Refinancing Criteria Divergence:**
    - **US GAAP (ASC 470):** Permits exclusion of short-term debt from current liabilities if refinanced on a long-term basis **before the financial statement issuance date**.
    - **IFRS (IAS 1):** Permits exclusion from current liabilities **ONLY IF** the refinancing agreement is fully executed **on or before the balance sheet date**.
- **Schedule XI Mandatory Statutory Liability Line Items:**
    1. Acceptances.
    2. Trade Creditors.
    3. Provision for Taxation.
    4. Proposed Dividend.
    5. Unclaimed Dividends.

#### 8.2 Amortisation Mechanics & Extinguishment Rules
- **Early Extinguishment Calculation Rules:**
    1. Compute Net Carrying Value (\text{NCV}): \text{NCV} = \text{Face Value} - \text{Unamortised Discount} + \text{Unamortised Premium} - \text{Unamortised Bond Issue Costs}
    2. Compute Reacquisition Price (\text{Total Cash Paid to Redeem Debt}, excluding accrued interest).
    3. Recognize Gain or Loss on Extinguishment: \text{Gain on Extinguishment} = \text{NCV} - \text{Reacquisition Price} \quad (\text{if } \text{NCV} > \text{Reacquisition Price}) \text{Loss on Extinguishment} = \text{Reacquisition Price} - \text{NCV} \quad (\text{if } \text{Reacquisition Price} > \text{NCV})
- **Bond Issue Costs Netting Mechanics:** Under ASC 835-30 and IFRS 9, bond issue costs (legal fees, underwriting commissions, printing) are **netted directly against the initial carrying amount** of the bond liability at issuance (increasing the effective discount or reducing the premium), rather than recorded as a separate deferred asset. This reduced initial carrying value automatically increases the effective-interest rate over the borrowing term.
- **Non-Symmetric Modification Rules (US GAAP ASC 470-60):** Under a term modification:
    - **Debtor Test (Undiscounted Cash Flows):** Compare pre-restructuring carrying value to total _undiscounted_ future cash flows. If undiscounted cash flows are less than carrying value, recognize a restructuring gain and set the new effective-interest rate to 0\%. If undiscounted cash flows exceed carrying value, recognize **no gain** and calculate a new effective-interest rate.
    - **Creditor Test (Discounted Cash Flows):** Compares carrying value to expected future cash flows **discounted at the loan's ORIGINAL effective-interest rate**, recognizing an immediate impairment loss charged to Bad Debt Expense.

#### 9.2 Statutory Equity Classifications & ASC 480 Rules
- **Schedule XI Statutory Capital Structure Breakdown & Mechanics:** Part I of Schedule XI mandates explicit disclosure of four distinct levels of corporate share capital:
    1. **Authorized Capital:** Total share capacity permitted by the corporate charter (_e.g._, 100,000 shares of BDT 10 par value = BDT 1,000,000).
    2. **Issued Capital:** Aggregate par value of shares offered to the public or investors.
    3. **Subscribed Capital:** Total par value of shares applied for by investors and formally allotted by the Board of Directors.
    4. **Paid-up Capital:** Total actual cash or consideration received for allotted shares.
    - _Mandatory Disclosures:_ Schedule XI Part I requires separate line disclosures under Paid-up Capital for:
        - Shares allotted as fully paid up pursuant to contracts without payment being received in cash (_non-cash allotments_).
        - Shares allotted as fully paid up by way of bonus shares (_bonus share issuances_).
- **Mandatorily Redeemable Preferred Stock (ASC 480 / IAS 32):** Preferred stock that incorporates an unconditional requirement for the issuer to redeem it at a specified date or upon an event certain to occur possesses the economic characteristics of debt. Under ASC 480 and IAS 32, it **MUST be classified as a Financial Liability** on the balance sheet, and its dividend payments are reported as **Interest Expense**.

#### 11.2 Bangladesh Statutory & BSEC Compliance Rules
- **Schedule XI, Part II Mandatory Income Statement Disclosures:**
    1. **Turnover & Sales:** Total gross turnover, disaggregated by major product categories showing quantities sold.
    2. **Managerial Remuneration:** Specific disclosure of all salaries, commissions, bonuses, perquisites, and pension benefits paid to managing directors and corporate executives.
    3. **Auditor Fee Itemised Breakdown:** Mandatory itemised disclosure of fees paid to statutory auditors across four legal sub-categories:
        - For statutory audit fees.
        - For taxation advisory services.
        - For other advisory or certification services.
        - For reimbursement of out-of-pocket expenses.
    4. **Industrial Capacity & Operational Production:** Mandatory statutory disclosure showing:
        - Licensed capacity.
        - Installed capacity.
        - Actual operational production realised during the fiscal year.
    5. **Foreign Exchange Outflows & Earnings:** Detailed disclosure of foreign currency spent (imports of raw materials, capital equipment) and foreign exchange earned (export sales, royalties).
- **BSEC Rule 12(2) Compliance Mandates for Publicly Listed Entities:**
    - **120-Day Submission Deadline:** Complete audited financial statement packages must be submitted to the BSEC and Stock Exchanges (DSE/CSE) within **120 days (4 months)** from fiscal year-end.
    - **Interim Reporting Timelines:** Half-yearly financial statements must be published within **45 days** of half-year end; quarterly reports must be published within **30 days** of quarter-end.
    - **Corporate Governance Compliance Report:** Listed entities must include a mandatory Corporate Governance Compliance Certificate executed by a practising Chartered Accountant (CA) or Cost & Management Accountant (CMA).

#### 12.2 Final Exam Pitfall Warnings & Terminology Selection Rules
**ACC 301 EXAMINATION TERMINOLOGY SELECTION DECISION RULES** Students must carefully assess the narrative context of every examination problem to determine the appropriate accounting terminology and format:
1. **US GAAP / Standard Text Context:** When an exam problem cites Weygandt, Kieso, US corporations, or US GAAP standards, students **MUST** apply US GAAP nomenclature (_e.g._, Net Income, Accounts Receivable, Allowance for Doubtful Accounts, Additional Paid-in Capital, Stockholders' Equity) and structure balance sheets in the **Order of Liquidity**.
2. **Bangladesh Statutory / Local Legal Context:** When an exam problem explicitly cites a Bangladeshi corporate entity, Companies Act 1994, Schedule XI, BSEC Rules, or BFRS standards, students **MUST** apply statutory terms (_e.g._, Profit and Loss Account, Sundry Debtors, Provision for Doubtful Debts, Share Premium, Share Capital & Reserves) and draft the Balance Sheet strictly under the **Order of Permanence**.
3. **Rule 1: Statutory Balance Sheet Layout (Order of Permanence)** When an exam question specifically requests the drafting of a Balance Sheet under **Schedule XI of the Companies Act, 1994**, students **MUST** apply the _Order of Permanence_. Present Share Capital and Reserves first under Capital and Liabilities, and present Fixed Assets (PP&E) first under Assets. Listing Cash first results in an immediate structural deduction.
4. **Rule 2: LIFO Prohibition Under IFRS / Local Context** Remember that LIFO is permitted **ONLY** under US GAAP. If an examination question specifies that the entity reports under IFRS, IAS 2, or Bangladesh statutory standards, LIFO is strictly prohibited. Calculate ending inventory and COGS using FIFO or Weighted-Average Cost.
5. **Rule 3: Loss Contingency vs. Provision Accrual Thresholds**
    - Under US GAAP (ASC 450), accrue a Loss Contingency if the loss is **Probable** (\sim 75\% \text{--} 80\%+). If a range is provided without a single best estimate, accrue the **MINIMUM** point of the range.
    - Under IFRS (IAS 37), accrue a Provision if the loss is **More Likely Than Not** (> 50\%). If a range is provided, accrue the **MIDPOINT (Expected Value)** of the range.
6. **Rule 4: Debt Refinancing Date Constraints** Short-term debt expected to be refinanced can be excluded from Current Liabilities under US GAAP if refinanced **before the financial statement issuance date**. Under IFRS (IAS 1), the refinancing agreement **MUST be executed on or before the balance sheet date**.
7. **Rule 5: Cash Flow Activity Divergence for Interest and Dividends** Under US GAAP defaults: Interest Paid = Operating; Interest Received = Operating; Dividends Received = Operating; Dividends Paid = Financing. Under IFRS (IAS 7), companies possess flexible policy choices (_e.g._, Interest Paid can be classified as Operating or Financing).
8. **Rule 6: Treasury Stock Classification** Treasury stock is **NEVER** an asset. Under all frameworks, Treasury Stock / Treasury Shares must be presented as a deduction from total equity (contra-equity account).
9. **Rule 7: Mandatorily Redeemable Preferred Stock** Under ASC 480 and IAS 32, preferred stock that is mandatorily redeemable at a fixed future date **MUST** be classified as a **Liability**, not Equity. Dividends paid on these shares are reported as **Interest Expense**.
10. **Rule 8: Property, Plant & Equipment Revaluation** The Revaluation Model (crediting Revaluation Surplus in Equity) is permitted under IFRS (IAS 16) and recognized locally, but is strictly prohibited under US GAAP, which mandates the historical Cost Model.
11. **Rule 9: Sundry Debtors Legal Sub-classifications & Netting** Schedule XI Part I questions require _Sundry Debtors_ to be categorized into three distinct legal lines: (1) Good & Fully Secured, (2) Good & Unsecured, and (3) Doubtful/Bad. Deduct the _Provision for Bad and Doubtful Debts_ directly on the face of the asset section from Sundry Debtors.
12. **Rule 10: Bond Issue Costs Netting Mechanics** Do not record bond issue costs as an intangible asset. Under ASC 835-30 and IFRS 9, net bond issue costs directly against the initial bond liability carrying amount at issuance.
