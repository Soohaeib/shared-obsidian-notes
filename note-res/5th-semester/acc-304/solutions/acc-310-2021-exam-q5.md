### (a)

#### (a_i)
##### Apportionment of Expenses by the Most Equitable Method
To apportion the factory overhead expenses among the four departments (Production Departments A, B, C and Service Department D), each cost item is allocated using the most equitable cost driver:
- **Rent ($6,000):** Apportioned on the basis of **Area (square ft)** in the ratio $1,500 : 1,100 : 900 : 500 = 15 : 11 : 9 : 5$ (Total parts = 40).
- **Depreciation of Plant ($9,000):** Apportioned on the basis of **Value of Plant** in the ratio $24,000 : 18,000 : 12,000 : 6,000 = 4 : 3 : 2 : 1$ (Total parts = 10).
- **Supervision ($12,000):** Apportioned on the basis of **No. of Employees** in the ratio $20 : 15 : 10 : 5 = 4 : 3 : 2 : 1$ (Total parts = 10).
- **Power ($1,600):** Apportioned on the basis of **Horsepower $\times$ Running Hours (HP-Hours)** in the ratio $2,400,000 : 1,000,000 : 200,000 : 0 = 12 : 5 : 1 : 0$ (Total parts = 18).
- **Repairs to Plant ($1,000):** Apportioned on the basis of **Value of Plant** in the ratio $4 : 3 : 2 : 1$ (Total parts = 10).
- **Light ($1,000):** Apportioned on the basis of **Light Points** in the ratio $40 : 30 : 20 : 10 = 4 : 3 : 2 : 1$ (Total parts = 10).
- **Fire Insurance ($1,500):** Apportioned on the basis of **Value of Stock** in the ratio $15,000 : 9,000 : 6,000 : 0 = 5 : 3 : 2 : 0$ (Total parts = 10).
- **Employees Liability Insurance ($600):** Apportioned on the basis of **Total Wages** in the ratio $6,000 : 4,000 : 3,000 : 2,000 = 6 : 4 : 3 : 2$ (Total parts = 15).

### (b)
#### Re-apportionment of Service Department Costs Using Simultaneous Equation Method
**Step 1: Express Service Department Costs as Simultaneous Linear Equations** Let $D$ represent the complete reciprocated overhead cost of **Department D**. Let $E$ represent the complete reciprocated overhead cost of **Department E**.

From the given service distribution percentages:

$$
D = 8,500 + 0.30 E \quad \text{--- (Equation 1)}
$$

$$
E = 10,500 + 0.20 D \quad \text{--- (Equation 2)}
$$

**Step 2: Solve the Simultaneous Equations** Substitute Equation 2 into Equation 1:

$$
D = 8,500 + 0.30(10,500 + 0.20 D)
$$

$$
D = 8,500 + 3,150 + 0.06 D
$$

$$
D - 0.06 D = 11,650
$$

$$
0.94 D = 11,650
$$

$$
