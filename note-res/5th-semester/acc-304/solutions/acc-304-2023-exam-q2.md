### (a)
#### Economic Order Quantity and Graphical Model
##### Meaning of Economic Order Quantity (EOQ)
Economic Order Quantity (EOQ) is the optimal order quantity a company should purchase to minimize total inventory costs, which consist of ordering costs and inventory carrying (holding) costs. It balances the cost of placing purchase orders against the cost of holding inventory in stock.

The mathematical formula for EOQ is given by:

$$
\text{EOQ} = \sqrt{\frac{2 \times U \times O}{S}}
$$

Where:
- $U = \text{Annual Usage / Demand in units}$
- $O = \text{Cost of placing an order}$
- $S = \text{Annual inventory carrying cost per unit}$
##### Graphical Representation of EOQ
In the graphical model of EOQ:
1. **Annual Ordering Cost Curve:** Slopes downward from left to right because as order size ($Q$) increases, fewer orders are placed per year, reducing total ordering costs.
2. **Annual Carrying Cost Line:** Slopes upward linearly from left to right because a larger order size ($Q$) increases the average inventory held ($\frac{Q}{2}$), thereby increasing total carrying costs.
3. **Total Annual Inventory Cost Curve:** Formed by adding ordering and carrying costs together. It forms a $U$-shaped curve.
4. **EOQ Point:** The point of intersection where the annual ordering cost line intersects the annual carrying cost line. At this exact point, total inventory cost is minimized.

#### (c_i)
##### Reorder Quantity (EOQ)
$$
\text{Reorder Quantity (EOQ)} = \sqrt{\frac{2 \times U \times O}{S}}
$$

$$
\text{EOQ} = \sqrt{\frac{2 \times 12,000 \times 100}{15}} = \sqrt{\frac{2,400,000}{15}} = \sqrt{160,000} = 400 \text{ units}
$$

> [!success] Final Answer: Reorder Quantity **Reorder Quantity (EOQ) = 400 units**

#### (c_ii)
##### Reorder Level
$$
\text{Reorder Level} = \text{Maximum Usage per Week} \times \text{Maximum Lead Time}
$$

$$
\text{Reorder Level} = 125 \text{ units/week} \times 6 \text{ weeks} = 750 \text{ units}
$$

> [!success] Final Answer: Reorder Level **Reorder Level = 750 units**

#### (c_iii)
##### Maximum Level
$$
\text{Maximum Level} = \text{Reorder Level} + \text{Reorder Quantity} - (\text{Minimum Usage per Week} \times \text{Minimum Lead Time})
$$

$$
\text{Maximum Level} = 750 + 400 - (65 \times 4) = 1,150 - 260 = 890 \text{ units}
$$

> [!success] Final Answer: Maximum Level **Maximum Level = 890 units**

#### (c_iv)
##### Minimum Level
$$
\text{Minimum Level} = \text{Reorder Level} - (\text{Normal Usage per Week} \times \text{Average Lead Time})
$$

$$
\text{Minimum Level} = 750 - (100 \times 5) = 750 - 500 = 250 \text{ units}
$$

> [!success] Final Answer: Minimum Level **Minimum Level = 250 units**

#### (c_v)
##### Average Stock Level
$$
\text{Average Stock Level} = \text{Minimum Level} + \frac{1}{2}(\text{Reorder Quantity})
$$

$$
\text{Average Stock Level} = 250 + \frac{400}{2} = 250 + 200 = 450 \text{ units}
