### (a)
#### (a_i)
##### Normal Rate of Wages
To compute the normal rate of wages per hour, equations for the factory costs of Worker A and Worker B are established based on their respective incentive schemes.

Let:
- $R =$ Normal hourly wage rate (in BDT)
- $M =$ Material cost per product (in BDT)

**Worker A (Rowan Premium Scheme):**
- Time Allowed ($S$) = $50\text{ hours}$
- Time Taken ($T_A$) = $30\text{ hours}$
- Time Saved ($S_A$) = $50 - 30 = 20\text{ hours}$


$$
\text{Rowan Bonus} = \left( \frac{\text{Time Saved}}{\text{Time Allowed}} \right) \times \text{Time Taken} \times \text{Wage Rate} = \left( \frac{20}{50} \right) \times 30 \times R = 12R
$$



$$
\text{Total Earnings of A } (E_A) = (T_A \times R) + \text{Bonus} = 30R + 12R = 42R
$$



$$
\text{Factory Overhead for A} = 30\text{ hours} \times \text{BDT } 5 = \text{BDT } 150
$$



$$
\text{Factory Cost of A} = M + E_A + \text{Overhead} = M + 42R + 150 = 3,490
$$



$$
M + 42R = 3,340 \quad \text{--- (Equation 1)}
$$


**Worker B (Halsey Premium Scheme - 50% Sharing):**
- Time Allowed ($S$) = $50\text{ hours}$
- Time Taken ($T_B$) = $40\text{ hours}$
- Time Saved ($S_B$) = $50 - 40 = 10\text{ hours}$


$$
\text{Halsey Bonus} = 50\% \times \text{Time Saved} \times \text{Wage Rate} = 0.50 \times 10 \times R = 5R
$$



$$
\text{Total Earnings of B } (E_B) = (T_B \times R) + \text{Bonus} = 40R + 5R = 45R
$$



$$
\text{Factory Overhead for B} = 40\text{ hours} \times \text{BDT } 5 = \text{BDT } 200
$$



$$
\text{Factory Cost of B} = M + E_B + \text{Overhead} = M + 45R + 200 = 3,600
$$



$$
M + 45R = 3,400 \quad \text{--- (Equation 2)}
$$


Subtracting Equation 1 from Equation 2:


$$
(M + 45R) - (M + 42R) = 3,400 - 3,340
$$



$$
3R = 60 \implies R = \text{BDT } 20\text{ per hour}
$$


> [!success] Final Answer: BDT 20 per hour

#### Labour Turnover Definition and Causes
##### Definition
Labour turnover refers to the rate of change in the labor force of an organization during a specified period of time. It reflects the proportion of workers who leave an organization relative to the average number of workers employed, serving as a measure of employee stability and organizational workforce mobility.
##### Causes of Labour Turnover
Labour turnover arises from various factors categorized into avoidable and unavoidable causes:
1. **Avoidable Causes:**
    - Low wages, unsatisfactory allowance levels, and poor financial incentives.
    - Bad working conditions and inadequate safety or welfare provisions.
    - Poor interpersonal relations among employees or between workers and management/supervisors.
    - Unsuitability of job placements and lack of promotion opportunities.
2. **Unavoidable Causes:**
    - Retirement due to old age or permanent ill health.
    - Death of employees.
    - Domestic constraints, personal family issues, or relocation.
    - Resignation for better career opportunities elsewhere.
    - Dismissal or discharge due to insubordination, misconduct, or inefficiency.
