### (a)
#### Definitions of Key Cost Concepts
1. **Cost Object:**
* A cost object is any item, activity, customer, product, service, project, or organizational segment for which a separate measurement of costs is desired.
* *Example:* A specific batch of products, a customer account, a manufacturing department, or a sales territory.
2. **Cost Driver:**
* A cost driver is any variable or factor (such as machine-hours, direct labor-hours, or number of setups) that causally affects costs—meaning a change in the level of the cost driver causes a proportional change in the total cost of a related cost object.
* *Example:* The number of machine hours worked is a cost driver for machine maintenance expenses.
3. **Direct Costs:**
* Direct costs are costs that are directly related to a specific cost object and can be traced to that cost object in an economically feasible (cost-effective) way.
* *Example:* The raw materials and assembly labor used in manufacturing a specific unit of furniture.
4. **Indirect Costs:**
* Indirect costs are costs that are related to a specific cost object but cannot be traced to it in an economically feasible way; instead, they are allocated to the cost object using a systematic cost-allocation method.
* *Example:* Factory rent, depreciation of plant equipment, and salary of the general factory supervisor.
5. **Period Costs:**
* Period costs are all costs in the income statement other than cost of goods sold. They are treated as expenses of the accounting period in which they are incurred because they are expected to benefit current revenues and cannot be allocated to inventory/production.
* *Example:* Selling, marketing, distribution, research and development, and administrative expenses.

#### (b_i)
##### Cost of Materials Used

$$
\text{Cost of Materials Used} = \text{Opening Raw Materials} + \text{Purchases} + \text{Carriage Inward} - \text{Ending Raw Materials}

---
#### (b_ii)

$$

#### (b_iii)

$$

$$



$$
\text{Works Cost} = \text{Prime Cost} + \text{Net Factory Overhead}

$$

#### (b_vii)
##### Sales

$$
