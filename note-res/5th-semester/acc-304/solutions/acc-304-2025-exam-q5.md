### (c)
#### Reconciliation of Operating Income Difference
The operating income under absorption costing is $\$8,000$ higher than under variable costing ($\$58,600 - \$50,600 = \$8,000$).
##### Explanation of Reconciliation
The difference arises solely because production ($100,000 \text{ units}$) exceeded sales ($80,000 \text{ units}$), causing $20,000 \text{ units}$ to be held in ending inventory.

Under absorption costing, fixed manufacturing overhead is capitalized into ending inventory as an asset at a rate of $\$0.40 \text{ per unit}$:


$$
\text{Fixed Overhead Allocated per Unit} = \frac{\$40,000 \text{ Total Fixed Manufacturing Overhead}}{100,000 \text{ Units Produced}} = \$0.40 \text{ per unit}
$$



$$
\text{Fixed Overhead Deferred in Ending Inventory} = 20,000 \text{ units} \times \$0.40 = \$8,000
$$


Under variable costing, the entire $\$40,000$ of fixed manufacturing overhead is expensed immediately in 2020. Under absorption costing, only $\$32,000$ ($80,000 \text{ units} \times \$0.40$) is included in Cost of Goods Sold, deferring $\$8,000$ to future periods within ending inventory.


$$
\text{Operating Income}_{\text{Absorption}} - \text{Operating Income}_{\text{Variable}} = (\text{Ending Inventory Units} - \text{Beginning Inventory Units}) \times \text{Fixed Overhead Rate}
$$



$$
\$58,600 - \$50,600 = (20,000 - 0) \times \$0.40 = \$8,000
$$
