#### Definition and Types of Overhead Cost
**Definition of Overhead Cost:** Overhead cost (also referred to as indirect manufacturing cost or factory overhead cost) consists of all manufacturing costs that are related to the cost object (such as work-in-process and finished goods) but cannot be traced to that cost object in an economically feasible way. It includes all plant-related manufacturing costs other than direct materials and direct manufacturing labor.

**Classification of Overhead Costs:** Overhead costs are classified into distinct categories based on behavior, function, and element:
1. **Classification by Behavior (Nature):**
    - **Fixed Overhead:** Indirect costs that remain unchanged in total within a relevant range of production regardless of changes in output volume (e.g., factory building rent, plant insurance, straight-line depreciation of machinery).
    - **Variable Overhead:** Indirect costs that change in total in direct proportion to changes in the level of production or activity (e.g., indirect materials, lubricants, factory electricity consumed by machines).
    - **Semi-Variable (Mixed) Overhead:** Indirect costs containing both a fixed baseline component and a variable usage component (e.g., plant maintenance service with a base monthly charge plus hourly service fees).
2. **Classification by Function:**
    - **Factory (Manufacturing) Overhead:** All indirect expenses incurred within the manufacturing plant (e.g., plant supervision, factory utilities, maintenance staff wages).
    - **Administrative Overhead:** Indirect costs associated with the general management and administration of the enterprise (e.g., executive salaries, office rent, legal fees).
    - **Selling and Distribution Overhead:** Indirect costs incurred to secure orders and deliver finished goods to customers (e.g., advertising, sales commissions, warehouse rent, shipping expenses).
3. **Classification by Element:**
    - **Indirect Materials:** Material items used in production processes that cannot be economically traced to specific units (e.g., cleaning solvents, lubricants, small fasteners).
    - **Indirect Labor:** Compensation paid to support personnel who do not directly transform raw materials (e.g., shop-floor supervisors, janitorial staff, plant security, idle time pay).
    - **Indirect Expenses:** Manufacturing expenses other than indirect materials and labor (e.g., property taxes on factory premises, plant depreciation, property insurance).

#### Distribution of Service Department Costs Using Algebraic Method
**Step 1: Express Service Department Costs as Simultaneous Linear Equations** Let $B$ represent the complete reciprocated cost of the **Boiler house**. Let $P$ represent the complete reciprocated cost of the **Pump room**.

From the given allocation percentages, the simultaneous equations are formulated as:

$$
B = 300,000 + 0.50 P \quad \text{--- (Equation 1)}
$$

$$
P = 60,000 + 0.05 B \quad \text{--- (Equation 2)}
$$

**Step 2: Solve the Simultaneous Linear Equations** Substitute Equation 2 into Equation 1:

$$
B = 300,000 + 0.50(60,000 + 0.05 B)
$$

$$
B = 300,000 + 30,000 + 0.025 B
$$

$$
B - 0.025 B = 330,000
$$

$$
0.975 B = 330,000
$$

$$
B = \frac{330,000}{0.975} = \$338,461.54 \quad \left(\text{exactly } \$ \frac{4,400,000}{13}\right)
$$

Substitute the value of $B$ back into Equation 2:

$$
P = 60,000 + 0.05(338,461.54)
$$

$$
P = 60,000 + 16,923.08 = \$76,923.08 \quad \left(\text{exactly } \$ \frac{1,000,000}{13}\right)
$$
