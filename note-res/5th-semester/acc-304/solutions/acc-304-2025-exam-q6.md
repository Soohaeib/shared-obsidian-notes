bal_val = 6000.0

transactions = [
    ('Jan 6', 'rec', 200, 12.50),
    ('Jan 10', 'rec', 400, 13.00),
    ('Jan 15', 'iss', 560, None),
    ('Jan 25', 'rec', 500, 14.00),
    ('Jan 27', 'iss', 400, None)

for date, ttype, qty, rate in transactions:
    if ttype == 'rec':
        bal_qty += qty
        bal_val += qty * rate
        avg_rate = bal_val / bal_qty
        print(f"{date}: Rec {qty} @ {rate} -> Bal: {bal_qty} units @ {avg_rate:.4f} = {bal_val:.2f}")

print(f"Total Cost of Materials Consumed (Moving Average): Tk. {total_issue_cost:.2f}")
print(f"Ending Inventory Cost (Moving Average): Tk. {bal_val:.2f}")

### (c)
#### Inventory Control Levels and EOQ Analysis
> [!abstract] Working Notes - Preliminary Parameters
> - **Normal Daily Usage:** $400 \text{ units/day}$
> - **Maximum Daily Usage:** $600 \text{ units/day}$
> - **Minimum Daily Usage:** $100 \text{ units/day}$
> - **Working Days per Year:** $250 \text{ days}$
> - **Annual Usage ($D$):** $400 \times 250 = 100,000 \text{ units}$
> - **Lead Time:** $8 \text{ days}$
> - **Ordering Cost per Order ($O$):** $\text{Tk. } 20.00$
> - **Carrying Cost per Unit per Year ($C$):** $\text{Tk. } 2.50 \times 10\% = \text{Tk. } 0.25 \text{ per unit/year}$

#### (c_i)
##### Economic Order Quantity (EOQ)

$$
\text{EOQ} = \sqrt{\frac{2 \times D \times O}{C}}
$$



$$
\text{EOQ} = \sqrt{\frac{2 \times 100,000 \times 20.00}{0.25}} = \sqrt{\frac{4,000,000}{0.25}} = \sqrt{16,000,000} = 4,000 \text{ units}
$$


> [!success] Final Answer: Economic Order Quantity **EOQ = 4,000 units**

#### (c_ii)
##### Safety Stock

$$
\text{Safety Stock} = (\text{Maximum Usage per Day} - \text{Normal Usage per Day}) \times \text{Lead Time}
$$



$$
\text{Safety Stock} = (600 - 400) \times 8 = 200 \times 8 = 1,600 \text{ units}
$$


> [!success] Final Answer: Safety Stock **Safety Stock = 1,600 units**

#### (c_iii)
##### Reorder Point

$$
\text{Reorder Point} = \text{Maximum Usage per Day} \times \text{Lead Time}
$$



$$
\text{Reorder Point} = 600 \times 8 = 4,800 \text{ units}
$$


_(Alternative formula check: $\text{Safety Stock} + (\text{Normal Usage per Day} \times \text{Lead Time}) = 1,600 + (400 \times 8) = 4,800 \text{ units}$)_

> [!success] Final Answer: Reorder Point **Reorder Point = 4,800 units**

#### (c_iv)
##### Normal Maximum Inventory

$$
\text{Normal Maximum Inventory} = \text{Reorder Point} + \text{EOQ} - (\text{Minimum Usage per Day} \times \text{Lead Time})
$$



$$
\text{Normal Maximum Inventory} = 4,800 + 4,000 - (100 \times 8) = 8,800 - 800 = 8,000 \text{ units}
$$


> [!success] Final Answer: Normal Maximum Inventory **Normal Maximum Inventory = 8,000 units**

#### (c_v)
##### Absolute Maximum Inventory

$$
\text{Absolute Maximum Inventory} = \text{Safety Stock} + \text{EOQ}
$$



$$
\text{Absolute Maximum Inventory} = 1,600 + 4,000 = 5,600 \text{ units}
$$


> [!success] Final Answer: Absolute Maximum Inventory **Absolute Maximum Inventory = 5,600 units**

#### (c_vi)
##### Average Normal Inventory

$$
\text{Average Normal Inventory} = \text{Safety Stock} + \frac{\text{EOQ}}{2}
$$



$$
\text{Average Normal Inventory} = 1,600 + \frac{4,000}{2} = 1,600 + 2,000 = 3,600 \text{ units}
$$


> [!success] Final Answer: Average Normal Inventory **Average Normal Inventory = 3,600 units**
