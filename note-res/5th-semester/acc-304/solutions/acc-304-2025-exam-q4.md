# Method 1: Daily Basis

# Normal hours: up to 8 hours
# Single overtime rate (Tk 10/hr): 9th hour (i.e. between 8 and 9 hours, so max 1 hour)
# Double overtime rate (Tk 20/hr): hours above 9 hours

daily_breakdown = []
total_normal_hrs_daily = 0
total_ot_single_hrs_daily = 0
total_ot_double_hrs_daily = 0

for day, hrs in hours.items():
    if hrs <= 8:
        norm = hrs
        ot_s = 0
        ot_d = 0
    elif hrs == 9:
        norm = 8
        ot_s = 1
        ot_d = 0
    else: # hrs > 9
        norm = 8
        ot_s = 1
        ot_d = hrs - 9
    
    total_normal_hrs_daily += norm
    total_ot_single_hrs_daily += ot_s
    total_ot_double_hrs_daily += ot_d
    daily_breakdown.append((day, hrs, norm, ot_s, ot_d))

wages_daily_normal = total_normal_hrs_daily * normal_rate

### (a)
#### Calculation of Rate per Hour Worked and Wages Payable under Alternative Bonus Schemes
> [!abstract] Working Notes
> 1. **Basic Parameters:**
>     - Standard Time ($T_s$) = $32\text{ hours}$
>     - Time Saved ($T_{\text{saved}}$) = $4\text{ hours}$
>     - Time Taken ($T_t$) = $T_s - T_{\text{saved}} = 32 - 4 = 28\text{ hours}$
>     - Award Rate per hour ($R$) = $\text{Tk. } 1.00$
> 2. **Scheme (i): Hourly Rate Increase Based on Percentage of Time Saved to Time Set**
>     - Percentage of time saved to time set (standard time):

$$
\text{Percentage Saved} = \left( \frac{\text{Time Saved}}{\text{Standard Time}} \right) \times 100 = \left( \frac{4}{32} \right) \times 100 = 12.5\%
$$

> - Increase in hourly rate:

$$
\text{Rate Increase} = \text{Award Rate} \times 12.5\% = \text{Tk. } 1.00 \times 0.125 = \text{Tk. } 0.125\text{ per hour}
$$

> - New rate per hour worked:

$$
\text{Rate per Hour Worked} = \text{Award Rate} + \text{Rate Increase} = \text{Tk. } 1.00 + \text{Tk. } 0.125 = \text{Tk. } 1.125\text{ per hour}
$$

> - Wages payable for time taken:

$$
\text{Wages Payable} = \text{Time Taken} \times \text{Rate per Hour Worked} = 28\text{ hours} \times \text{Tk. } 1.125 = \text{Tk. } 31.50
$$

> 3. **Scheme (ii): Bonus Based on Efficiency Level Achieved**
>     - Efficiency achieved by employee:

$$
\text{Efficiency \%} = \left( \frac{\text{Standard Time}}{\text{Time Taken}} \right) \times 100 = \left( \frac{32}{28} \right) \times 100 = 114.2857\% \approx 114.29\%
$$

> - Efficiency in excess of $100\%$ standard efficiency:

$$
\text{Excess Efficiency \%} = 114.2857\% - 100\% = 14.2857\%
$$

> - Total bonus percentage on award rate:
>     - Bonus for $100\%$ efficiency = $10\%$
>     - Further bonus for excess efficiency ($1\%$ for each $1\%$ excess) = $14.2857\%$

$$
\text{Total Bonus \%} = 10\% + 14.2857\% = 24.2857\%
$$

> - Bonus rate per hour:

$$
\text{Bonus per Hour} = \text{Award Rate} \times 24.2857\% = \text{Tk. } 1.00 \times 0.242857 = \text{Tk. } 0.242857\text{ per hour}
$$

> - Effective rate per hour worked:

$$
\text{Rate per Hour Worked} = \text{Award Rate} + \text{Bonus per Hour} = \text{Tk. } 1.00 + \text{Tk. } 0.242857 = \text{Tk. } 1.242857 \approx \text{Tk. } 1.2429\text{ per hour}
$$

> - Wages payable for time taken:

$$
\text{Wages Payable} = \text{Time Taken} \times \text{Rate per Hour Worked} = 28\text{ hours} \times \text{Tk. } 1.242857 = \text{Tk. } 34.80
$$

$$
\begin{array}{lrr} \hline \textbf{Particulars} & \textbf{Scheme (i)} & \textbf{Scheme (ii)} \\ \hline \text{Standard Time (hours)} & 32.00 & 32.00 \\ \text{Time Saved (hours)} & 4.00 & 4.00 \\ \text{Time Taken (hours)} & 28.00 & 28.00 \\ \text{Award Rate per Hour (Tk.)} & 1.00 & 1.00 \\ \text{Increase / Bonus per Hour (Tk.)} & 0.1250 & 0.2429 \\ \text{Rate per Hour Worked (Tk.)} & 1.1250 & 1.2429 \\ \hline \textbf{Wages Payable (Tk.)} & \underline{\underline{31.50}} & \underline{\underline{34.80}} \\ \hline \end{array}
$$

> [!success] Final Answer:
> - **Scheme (i):** Rate per hour worked = **Tk. 1.125**; Wages payable = **Tk. 31.50**
> - **Scheme (ii):** Rate per hour worked = **Tk. 1.2429** (or Tk. 1.24); Wages payable = **Tk. 34.80**

$$
\text{Labour Turnover Rate} = \left( \frac{\text{Total Separations}}{\text{Average Number of Workers}} \right) \times 100

$$
