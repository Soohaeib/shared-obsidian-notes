### (a)
#### Overhead Definition and Functional Classification
**Definition of Overhead:** Overhead (also referred to as indirect cost or factory overhead) consists of all manufacturing and non-manufacturing costs that are related to a cost object but cannot be traced directly to that cost object in an economically feasible way. Instead of direct tracing, overhead costs must be allocated or apportioned to cost centers or products using systematic cost-allocation bases.

**Functional Classification of Overhead Costs:** Overhead costs are classified based on the functional division or major activity of the enterprise in which they are incurred:
1. **Factory / Manufacturing Overhead:**
    - **Definition:** All indirect costs incurred within the production plant or factory environment from raw material stage to finished goods completion.
    - **Examples:** Factory building rent, depreciation of plant and machinery, plant supervisor salaries, indirect materials (lubricants, cleaning rags), and factory power/utilities.
2. **Administrative Overhead:**
    - **Definition:** Indirect costs associated with the overall management, executive control, direction, and administration of an organization.
    - **Examples:** Office rent, secretarial and executive salaries, legal fees, general office utility bills, and accounting department expenses.
3. **Selling Overhead:**
    - **Definition:** Indirect expenses incurred to promote, create, and maintain customer demand and secure orders.
    - **Examples:** Sales force salaries, sales commission, advertising campaigns, showroom expenses, and sales catalog printing.
4. **Distribution Overhead:**
    - **Definition:** Indirect expenses incurred from the time finished goods are completed in the factory until they reach the final customer.
    - **Examples:** Finished goods warehouse rent, transport/freight-out expenses, delivery van maintenance, and packaging for transit.
