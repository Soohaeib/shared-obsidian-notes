## 1. Architectural Overview & Comparative Frameworks

This reference guide provides a technical, cross-framework terminology lookup and decision framework for **ACC 301 Intermediate Accounting**. It bridges the three distinct accounting regimes evaluated in the course:

1. **US GAAP (FASB ASC):** Rules-based framework established by the Financial Accounting Standards Board (FASB), codified in the FASB Accounting Standards Codification (FASB ASC).
2. **IFRS (IAS / IFRS Standards):** Principles-based international standards issued by the International Accounting Standards Board (IASB), representing the primary textbook norm (*Weygandt, Kieso, Warfield, Intermediate Accounting IFRS 3rd Edition*).
3. **Bangladesh Statutory Framework:** Mandated by Parliament under **The Companies Act, 1994 (Schedule XI)** and enforced by the **Bangladesh Securities and Exchange Commission (BSEC)** under the **Securities and Exchange Rules, 1987 (Rule 12(2))**.

## 2. Structural Framework Comparison

| Financial Reporting Dimension | US GAAP (FASB ASC) | IFRS (IAS / IFRS Standards) | Bangladesh Statutory Framework (Companies Act 1994 / BSEC) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Orientation** | Rules-based (detailed, specific prescriptive guidelines). | Principles-based (simpler, flexible general frameworks). | Statutory legal mandate enforced by RJSC and BSEC for listed entities. |
| **Balance Sheet Presentation Order** | **Order of Liquidity:** Liquid items (Cash, Current Assets) presented first. | **Reverse Liquidity / Flexible:** Typically presents Non-Current Assets and Equity/Long-Term Liabilities first. | **Order of Permanence:** Mandated by Schedule XI Part I. Share Capital and PP&E presented first, descending to liquid items. |
| **Conceptual Framework Status** | Non-authoritative: SFACs guide FASB but do not override the Codification. | Authoritative: IAS 8 mandates applying the Conceptual Framework if no specific standard exists. | Statutory Act overrides accounting standards in cases of conflict; legal schedules prescribe explicit layouts. |
| **Cash Flow Statement Mandate** | Mandatory under ASC 230 for all complete sets. | Mandatory under IAS 7 for all reporting entities. | Mandatory for listed public entities under BSEC Rule 12(2); optional for small non-listed private firms. |

## 4. Bangladesh Statutory Presentation Rules (Schedule XI & BSEC)

### 4.1 Schedule XI Part I: Balance Sheet Layout (*Order of Permanence*)

Under **Schedule XI, Part I of the Companies Act, 1994**, corporate balance sheets in Bangladesh must follow the **Order of Permanence** (the reverse of US GAAP liquidity order):

```
CAPITAL AND LIABILITIES
1. Share Capital (Authorized, Issued, Subscribed, Paid-up)
2. Reserves and Surplus (Capital Reserves, Share Premium, General Reserve, P&L Surplus)
3. Secured Loans (Debentures, Secured Bank Loans against hypothecation)
4. Unsecured Loans (Short-Term Borrowings, Deposits)
5. Current Liabilities and Provisions (Acceptances, Trade Creditors, Provision for Taxation, Proposed Dividend, Unclaimed Dividends)

PROPERTY AND ASSETS
1. Fixed Assets (PP&E: Land, Buildings, Plant & Machinery, Vehicles; Gross Cost less Cumulative Depreciation)
2. Investments (Government Securities, Shares/Debentures in Subsidiaries)
3. Current Assets, Loans, and Advances
   A. Current Assets (Inventories: Raw Materials/WIP/Finished Goods/Spares; Sundry Debtors; Cash & Bank Balances)
   B. Loans and Advances (Prepayments, Employee Advances)
4. Miscellaneous Expenditure (Unwritten-off Preliminary Expenses, Underwriting Commission)
```

### 4.2 Schedule XI Part II: Profit & Loss Account Minimum Line Disclosures
1. **Turnover & Sales:** Gross turnover disaggregated by major product line with quantities.
2. **Raw Material Consumption:** Quantities and valuations of major raw materials consumed, disaggregated into **imported vs. indigenous (locally sourced)** materials.
3. **Managerial Remuneration:** Explicit itemized breakdown of salaries, perquisites, commissions, bonuses, and pension contributions paid to directors and senior executives.
4. **Auditor Fee Disaggregation:** Mandatory disclosure of statutory auditor fees split across 4 legal sub-categories: (a) Audit fees, (b) Tax advisory, (c) Certification/other services, (d) Out-of-pocket expenses.
5. **Capacity Utilization:** Licensed capacity, installed capacity, and actual operational production realized during the fiscal year.

### 4.3 BSEC Rule 12(2) Compliance Mandates
* **120-Day Filing Deadline:** Publicly listed corporations must submit complete audited financial statements to BSEC and Stock Exchanges (DSE/CSE) within **120 days (4 months)** of fiscal year-end.
* **Interim Reporting Timelines:** Half-yearly statements published within **45 days**; quarterly reports within **30 days**.
* **Corporate Governance Code:** Mandatory Corporate Governance Compliance Certificate issued by a practicing Chartered Accountant (CA) or Cost & Management Accountant (CMA).

## 5. Summary Examination Decision Rules & Pitfall Warnings

1. **Rule 1: Statutory Balance Sheet Layout (Order of Permanence):** When an exam question specifies a Bangladeshi entity or Schedule XI of the Companies Act 1994, draft the Balance Sheet strictly under the *Order of Permanence* (Share Capital & PP&E first, Cash last). Presenting Cash first results in an immediate mark deduction.
2. **Rule 2: LIFO Prohibition:** LIFO is permitted **only** under US GAAP. If an exam problem cites IFRS, IAS 2, or Bangladesh statutory standards, LIFO is strictly prohibited. Use FIFO or Weighted-Average Cost.
3. **Rule 3: Provision vs. Contingency Accrual Thresholds:** Under US GAAP (ASC 450), accrue if loss is *Probable* (~75%–80%+) and accrue the *minimum* of a range. Under IFRS (IAS 37), accrue if loss is *More Likely Than Not* (>50%) and accrue the *midpoint / expected value* of a range.
4. **Rule 4: Debt Refinancing Execution Date:** Short-term debt can be reclassified as non-current under US GAAP if refinanced before statement issuance date. Under IFRS (IAS 1), the refinancing agreement **must be fully executed on or before the balance sheet date**.
5. **Rule 5: Convertible Bond Bifurcation:** Under US GAAP, convertible bonds are recorded 100% as debt. Under IFRS (IAS 32), convertible bonds **must be bifurcated** into a debt liability component and an equity conversion option component (Share Premium—Conversion Equity) using the *With-and-Without Method*.
6. **Rule 6: Treasury Shares:** Treasury shares are **never** an asset. They must be presented as a deduction from Total Equity (contra-equity account). No gain or loss is ever recognized in Profit or Loss on treasury share purchases, sales, or retirements.
