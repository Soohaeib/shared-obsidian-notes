How to use this note

This document maps your 5 courses to the specific textbooks provided. Use the tables to guide your daily reading.

  • Chapters & Sections to Read: Tells you exactly which heading/lesson to focus on.
  • Omit / Skip: Tells you what to ignore so you don't waste time on irrelevant material (e.g., US-specific tax laws, matrix algebra, complex appendices).
  • Local Supplements: Indicates where you need to rely on your professor's class notes or local Bangladeshi laws.

ACC 301: Intermediate Accounting

Core Textbook: Intermediate Accounting by Kieso, Weygandt, Warfield Syllabus Coverage: ~90%

US GAAP vs. IFRS

The textbook heavily references US GAAP/FASB. Always focus on the underlying accounting mechanics and skip any sections explicitly detailing LIFO (banned under IFRS) or US tax codes.

ACC 303: Advanced Statistical Techniques

Core Textbooks: Basic Econometrics by Gujarati AND Research Methodology by Kothari Syllabus Coverage: 100%

Textbook Split

Topics 1-5 belong to classical statistics (Kothari). Topics 6-10 belong to Econometrics/Regression (Gujarati).

Core Textbook: Cost Accounting: A Managerial Emphasis by Horngren, Datar, Rajan Syllabus Coverage: ~80%

Missing Content Alert

Horngren does not cover the historical Incentive Wage Plans (Taylor, Merrick, Gantt, etc.) or Bangladesh Cost Accounting Standards (BCAS). You must use local books (e.g., Jain & Narang or Polimeni) for Topic 5 wage plans.

ACC 305: Auditing and Assurance

Core Textbook: Auditing and Assurance Services by Messier, Glover, Prawitt Syllabus Coverage: ~85%

Approach to Auditing

Do not memorize the textbook word-for-word. Focus on the core logic: Why are we testing this? Which management assertion does this test prove?
