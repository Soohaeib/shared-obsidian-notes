  • Total initial investment: USD 11,000,000

  • The project life is 5 years.

Operating Information:

  • Expected annual sales revenue: USD 6,000,000
  • Annual operating costs (excluding depreciation and interest): USD 3,000,000

The project will be financed as follows:

  • 40% debt and 60% equity
  • Loan amount will carry an interest rate of 10% per year

Additional Information:

Required:
