Auditing and Assurance

Institution: University of Chittagong | Department: Department of Accounting Program: BBA 5th Semester Standard Format: 4 Hours | 75 Marks | Answer any FIVE questions sequentially/serially.

2025 Final Examination

Tracking Ref: Course Code ACC-305 | BBA 5th Semester

Question 1

  • (a) Define ‘Assurance Engagement’ and explain the key elements of an assurance engagement. [04]
  • (b) Differentiate between reasonable and limited assurance, can an audit of financial statements provide absolute assurance? Justify your answer with appropriate reasons. [04]
  • (c) Describe the meaning of the “expectations gap” in the context of auditing. Suggest appropriate steps that may help in minimizing the expectations gap between auditors and users of financial statements. [04]
  • (d) What is “Lowballing”? Point out the factors that an auditor should consider before accepting an audit engagement. [03]

Question 2

In professional audit practice, it is important to agree on the terms of engagement before any assignments are taken up. This ensures a clear understanding between the auditor and the client. In such a context, you are required to:

  • (a) Examine the key components of an audit engagement letter. [06]
  • (b) Evaluate the factors to be considered during the assignment of the audit engagement team. [04]
  • (c) Distinction among appointment letter, proposal letter, and engagement letter. Discuss the conditions and stages at which they are prepared and state the objectives of each. [05]

Question 3

  • (a) Briefly discuss the duties of auditors according to Section 213 of the Companies Act, 1994. [05]
  • (b) An auditor discovers a material misstatement in a client’s financial statements but is under pressure from the client to overlook it, as it could jeopardize a significant contract. In this scenario, how should the auditor respond to this situation, considering the ethical standards for professional accountants? [05]
  • (c) State the objectives, powers, and functions of the Financial Reporting Council under sections 7 and 8 of the Financial Reporting Act, 2015. [05]

Question 4

  • (a) What is audit risk? What are the different types of audit risk? [03]
  • (b) Briefly explain the components of the internal control system as set out in ISA 315, ‘Identifying and Assessing the Risks of Material Misstatement’. [05]
  • (c) Define the term ‘Assertion’ from the perspective of a financial audit. Describe the assertions related to ‘revenue’ and ‘receivables’ for conducting a financial audit. [07]

Question 5

  • (a) In accordance with ISA 500-Audit Evidence, explain the meaning of ‘sufficiency’ and ‘appropriateness’ of audit evidence, and discuss the various audit procedures for obtaining such evidence. [05]

  • (b)

    Your firm XYZ & Co. Chartered Accountants, has been appointed as the auditor of Merry Company Limited (MCL) for the financial year ended 31 March 2025. You have been assigned as the engagement manager.

    During discussions with management, you were informed that MCL has recently received a significant number of requests for credit notes due to customers alleging that goods were received in poor condition. Therefore, Management of MCL intends to strengthen controls over the dispatch of goods and confirmation of their condition upon delivery.

    Again, you were informed that the directors of MCL have recently discovered that the store manager has been committing fraud. As part of her responsibilities, she was in charge of ordering inventory. She created fictitious purchase orders to non-existent suppliers, recorded false goods received notes for deliveries that never occurred, and submitted fake supplier invoices to the accounts department for payment. These invoices were subsequently paid by the company.

    MCL has also experienced a history of inventory theft. As a result, the directors have decided to strengthen the internal control system over the inventory ordering process to ensure that only goods genuinely required for business purposes are purchased.

    • i. Identify the audit risks associated with the dispatch and recording of delivered goods at MCL and suggest appropriate audit procedures to address the identified risks in relation to the dispatch and recording of goods.
    • ii. Identify the audit risks associated with the ordering of inventories for MCL and suggest appropriate audit procedures to address the identified risks in relation to the ordering of inventories. [10]

Question 6

  • (a)

    You are the auditor of Meghna Vehicle PLC, whose main activity is to buy and sell of various types of motor vehicles. During the current year the company has suffered a significant fall in sales and abnormal increase of finance cost which has led to a net loss. The company is also experiencing cash flow problems. The draft financial statements show a loss for the current year but the forecast indicates a report to profitability in the upcoming financial year, as the directors are optimistic about the injection of sufficient amount of fresh capital.

    • i. Describe the auditor’s responsibilities in relation to management’s assessment of the entity’s ability to continue as a going concern.
    • ii. Identify the events or conditions that may cast significant doubt on an entity’s ability to continue as a going concern. [08]

  • (b) There are certain general matters that are required to be included in a management representation letter. In accordance with ISA 580 – Written Representations, describe these general matters that management is expected to confirm in the representation letter. [03]

  • (c) Briefly explain the contents of an auditor's report in accordance with ISA 700 – Forming an Opinion and Reporting on Financial Statements. [04]

Question 7

  • (a) A modified audit opinion is issued when the auditor concludes, based on the audit evidence obtained, that the financial statements as a whole are not free from material misstatement. In cases of material misstatement, there are two types of modified audit opinions that may be expressed. Describe these two types of audit opinions. [05]
  • (b) A modified audit opinion is issued when the auditor is unable to obtain sufficient appropriate audit evidence to conclude that the financial statements as a whole are free from material misstatement. In situations involving a limitation of scope, two types of modified audit opinions may be expressed. Describe these two types of audit opinions. [04]
  • (c) Define the following terms as used in the Independent Auditor’s Report: [06]
    • Key Audit Matters (KAM)
    • Emphasis of Matter paragraph
    • Other Matter paragraph

He tries to operate on the lowest possible costs and sees internal control as himself and his factory managers. The company has recently moved into the production of mass-produced food item and expected that they will grab a large market share. They have an audit committee (not liked by Mr. X) but no internal audit department.

  • (a) What advantages might accrue to the company if they set up an internal audit department? [04]

Question 1

Mr. Shreyaan is the auditor of XYZ company. During the audit of 2021 annual report, one branch office of XYZ company was destroyed by fire. Because of the fire, Mr. Shreyaan is unable to find out some sales invoices (about 2% of total sales) and there is no backup in the company. Without the invoices, Mr. Shreyaan is also unable to contact the customers for confirmation purposes. He has access to the remaining 98% of invoices, and he believes there is no material misstatement in these invoices.

  • (a) In the above case, which type of audit opinion Mr. Shreyaan would issue and why? [06]
  • (b) What do limited and negative assurance mean? [03]
  • (c) Discuss the most common types of assurance services. [06]

The auditor is satisfied that controls over the account registration process exist and the control design is adequate. However, the auditor finds that in some cases, customers who have a balance over approved credit limit can continue to purchase microwave successfully before they get approval from senior manager.
